01 / Sociology & research

My academic motivations center on material injustice, institutional harm, and the social production of knowledge.

Current 1st-year student in the Sociology Ph.D. program at Portland State University ↗.

B.A. in Sociology with a Certificate in Social Science Data Analysis at Western Washington University (2024).

Research interests

Psychedelics & substance use; public health & health disparities; quantitative methods & demography; deviance & social control; social movements & political change; cognition & consciousness; epistemology & the sociology of knowledge/science.

Publications & active projects

Photo Portraiture Enhances Empathy for Birds with Potential Benefits for Conservation and Sustainability ↗

Whitley et al. · Sustainability · 2025 · Published

Experimental research on how different representations of birds affect human empathy, with potential implications for conservation communication. Project website ↗

Tripping Hazard: Psychedelic User Typologies and Differential Risks of Health-Related Outcomes ↗

Manuscript in preparation · Preregistered research

Quantitative research using latent class analysis to study patterns of psychedelic use and differences in health-related outcomes.

“...normally they come for removal, and they go back with a new implant inserted”: Implant Removal and Immediate Reinsertion in Rwanda

Schwandt et al. · Submitted to Contraception and Reproductive Medicine - under peer review

Qualitative public health research on family planning programs in Rwanda using in-depth interviews and focus groups. Data collected Summer 2024. Funded by the National Science Foundation.

02 / Music & audio

I am a musician, producer, and audio engineer. My creative practice spans composition/arrangement, experimental sound design, live performance, and studio recording. I play drum set, percussion, keyboards, and synthesizers.

My solo music draws on film and video game soundtracks, ambient and electronic music, and conceptual storytelling. Explore selected solo releases and collaborations below; the full solo catalog is on Bandcamp ↗.

Solo albums & projects

Eco Fusion: 2019-24 Revisited ↗ 2024

A compilation/rework/remaster of electronic instrumental tracks (new and old). Each track invokes a sense of environment or ecology.

Organism ↗ 2022 / 2025 remaster

A long-form ambient electronic experience. Different versions of the same shared musical structure at different tempos; randomized modulation gives each version a different sonic character (like a living Organism).

Permutation 2: Temple of Revolving Doors↗ 2023

The second installment of the Permutation Series.Read the project explanation ↗

Permutation (The Permutation Series 1)↗ 2023

An experimental electronic compositions structured around musical permutation and non-linear listenting. Read the project explanation ↗

Mtetezi ↗ 2023

A hardware-only (mainly Elektron) instrumental electronic performance. Captured as one continuous studio take with no edits.

Betula Papyrifera ↗ 2022

A hardware-only (mainly Elektron) instrumental electronic performance. Captured as one continuous studio take with no edits.

The Relentless Beauty of Motion ↗ 2021

Single-take improvisations using only electronic music hardware (e.g., Bass Station II & Elektron boxes). The live performance imperfections are integrated, not rejected.

To Exist Without Name ↗ 2021

Ambient soundscapes incorporating field recordings from my time living in St. George, Utah and hiking in the surrounding area.

Blood Disk ↗ 2019

A score for a fictional video game. The game-world is a cyberpunk/horror dystopia where immortality is achieved through bio-digital technology.

The Ballad of Pleistocene ↗ 2018

An instrumental concept album with electronic, soundtrack, and jazz-fusion influences. This album explores human-kind's metaphysical evolution and the costs of this transformation.

Tantalus ↗ 2017 / 2019 remaster

An instrumental electronic composition inspired by the Greek myth of Tantalus.

Amarnth ↗ 2018

A conceptual album meditating on life/death; an expression of my coping with the death of my grandfather Keith Elvington (RIP).
